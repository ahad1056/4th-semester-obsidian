Class Notes: Solid State Electronics

📌 Main Topics

- Energy band diagram (Related Model)
- Material Characterization

🔬 Characterization Techniques

- Electrical: Mentioned as a primary method.
- Optical: Uses XRD (X-ray Diffraction) and Bragg's law to find material structure.

⚡ Energy Levels & Vacuum Diagram

- Work function: Represented as the energy gap between the Fermi level (EF) and the Vacuum level.
- Electron affinity (χ): Represented as the energy gap between the Conduction band (Ec) and the Vacuum level.
- Energy Levels Shown: Vacuum, Ec (Conduction band), EF (Fermi level), and Ev (Valence band).
- Diagrams also mention Ecm and Evm / EBM.

📱 Additional Concepts & References

- Intrinsic semiconductor
- The relationship or transition from MOSFET to MOS capacitor
- Fermi dirac distribution
- Reference Book: B. Streetman

## Class Notes: Research Tools and Methodology (Nov 12)

### Research Software Tools
- **VESTA:** The notebook specifically notes this software is for "Crystal structure" . It is a 3D visualization program used by researchers for structural models, volumetric data, and crystal morphologies. 
  - Link: https://jp-minerals.org/vesta/en/
- **Mendeley:** Listed in the notes as a "citation manager" . It is also written on the whiteboard . Researchers use it to store, organize, and format references for their research papers.
  - Link: https://www.mendeley.com/
- **Zotero:** Also listed as a citation manager  and mentioned on the whiteboard alongside Mendeley . It is a free, open-source alternative to Mendeley used to collect and organize research citations.
  - Link: https://www.zotero.org/

## Research Databases and Websites
- **IEEE Xplore:** Written on the whiteboard . This is a digital library and research database providing access to articles in computer science, electrical engineering, and electronics.
  - Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/
- **Sci-hub:** Highlighted in a box in the notebook  and written on the whiteboard . It is a shadow library website that researchers often use to bypass paywalls and download academic papers for free.
  - Link: https://sci-hub.se/ (Note: domain extensions frequently change)
