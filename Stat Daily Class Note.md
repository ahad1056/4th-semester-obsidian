## 📝 Summary

**1. Frequency Distribution:**
- The notes demonstrate how to create a frequency distribution table by determining the class interval, frequency ($f_i$), mid-value ($x$), and cumulative frequency ($cf$)[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
- Sturges' formula for calculating the number of classes ($k$) is given: 
  $$ k = 1 + 3.225 \log_{10}(N) $$[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)
- The formula for finding the Class Interval ($I$) is: 
  $$ I = \frac{\text{Range}}{k} $$[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)
- The concepts of "closed interval" and "open interval" are also mentioned[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

**2. Mean:**
- The formula for calculating the mean of grouped data is provided as:
  $$ \bar{x} = \frac{\sum fx}{\sum f} $$[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)
- A solved example shows $\sum fx = 87$ and $\sum f = 15$, resulting in a mean of $5.8$[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).

**3. Median:**
- The algorithm for finding the median class depends on the total observation: use position $\frac{n}{2}$ for even numbers, and position $\frac{n+1}{2}$ for odd numbers[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
- The main formula for calculating the median is:
  $$ Me = L + \frac{\frac{N}{2} - cf}{f} \times c $$[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)
  *(Here, $L$ = lower limit, $cf$ = cumulative frequency just above the median class, $f$ = frequency of the median class, and $c$ = class interval)[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).*

**4. Quartile & Quantile:**
- The formula for calculating the $i$-th Quartile ($Q_i$) is:
  $$ Q_i = L + \frac{\frac{iN}{4} - cf}{f_{Qi}} \times c $$[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)
- A diagram illustrates that the 2nd quartile ($Q_2$) is equal to the median[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).
- The topics of "Inter quartile Range" and "Quartile deviation" are listed[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).
- The "Quantile family" is categorized under central tendency measurement[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).
