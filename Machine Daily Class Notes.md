- ANIMATION OF MAGNETISM
 -  > [!abstract] Magnetic Origin of Electric Current
> - **Microscopic Origin:** Drifting electrons ($I = n A v_d q$) generate concentric circular magnetic field lines around the conductor ($B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$).
> - **Right-Hand Rule:** Thumb points along conventional current ($I$); fingers curl along the magnetic field ($\vec{B}$).
> - **Relativistic Origin:** Magnetism is electrostatics viewed from a moving reference frame. Lorentz length contraction alters the relative spacing of positive ions vs. drifting electrons, producing a net electrostatic force that appears as magnetism in the lab frame.!
