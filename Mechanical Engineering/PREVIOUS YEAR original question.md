## **SECTION –A**

**1.** `[(2+3+2.5+1.5=9)]`
a. Write the applications where the principle of thermodynamics is used. Give the Clausius statement for the first law of thermodynamics.
b. Show that for an adiabatic change in perfect gas, $PV^\gamma = \text{constant}$, where the symbols have their usual meaning.
c. Define critical temperature and internal energy. Mention the Conditions for isothermal process
d. Air is compressed adiabatically to half its volume. Calculate the change in its temperature.

**2.** `[(2+3+2.5+1.5=9)]`
a. Write about cyclic process and heat reservoir.
b. Give the basic description of Carnot ideal engine.
c. Give the physical significance of entropy. Explain the concept of heat death of universe.
d. A Carnot engine working between a source at temperature $27^\circ\text{C}$ and a sink $-73^\circ\text{C}$ delivers $300\text{ calories}$ of heat to the latter in one cycle. Calculate the work performance in joules by the engine per cycle.

**3.** `[(1.5+2+3+2.5=9)]`
a. What is a boiler? Give the classification of boilers according to i) axis of the shell and ii) heat source.
b. What do you mean by boiler accessories? Write the operation of super heater and air preheater.
c. Give a neat sketch of a locomotive boiler with its applications.
d. A coal fired boiler plant consumes $400\text{ kg}$ of coal per hour. The boiler evaporates $3200\text{ kg}$ of water at $44.5^\circ\text{C}$ into superheated steam at a pressure of $12\text{ bar}$ and $274.5^\circ\text{C}$. If the caloric value of fuel is $32,760\text{ kJ/kg}$ coal, determine a) equivalent evaporation "from and at $100^\circ\text{C}$" and b) thermal efficiency of the boiler.

**4.** `[(2.5+3+2.5+1=9)]`
a. What do you mean by scavenging? Explain the different types of scavenging.
b. Describe the working principle of a four-stroke diesel engine by sketching the sequence of events in the cylinder.
c. Draw the schematic diagram of the coil ignition system used for the petrol engine and explain.
d. Write the advantages of four-stroke engines over two-strokes engines.

## **SECTION –B**

**5.** `[(2+1.5+3.5+2=9)]`
a. What do you mean by thermal conductivity? Describe the concept of thermal resistance.
b. Write about the purpose and applications of thermal insulation.
c. Define the term critical thickness of insulation. Derive the equation for critical radius for cylinder.
d. The average rate at which energy is conducted outward through the ground surface at a place is $50.0\text{ mW/m}^2$, and the average thermal conductivity of the near-surface rocks is $2.00\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}$. Assuming surface temperature of $20.0^\circ\text{C}$, find the temperature at a depth of $25.0\text{ km}$.

**6.** `[(2+2+2+3=9)]`
a. Define ton of refrigeration. Briefly describe the magnetic refrigeration.
b. Show the schematic diagram of a vapor-compression refrigeration system.
c. What is a refrigerant? Write the properties and uses of ammonia ($\text{NH}_3$) refrigerant.
d. A cold storage plant is required to store $20\text{ tons}$ of fish. The fish is supplied at a temperature of $30^\circ\text{C}$. The specific heat of fish above freezing point is $2.93\text{ kJ/kg-K}$ and that below freezing point is $1.26\text{ kJ/kg-K}$. The fish is stored in cold storage which is maintained at $-8^\circ\text{C}$. The freezing point of the fish is $-4^\circ\text{C}$ and the latent heat is $235\text{ kJ/kg}$. If the plant requires $75\text{ kW}$ to drive it, then find
   i) The capacity of the plant and
   ii) Time taken to achieve the cooling
   Assume actual C.O.P of the plant as $0.3$ of the Carnot C.O.P.

**7.** `[(1.5+2+3+2.5=9)]`
a. Define the terms: Relative humidity, dehumidification and sensible heat factor.
b. Write about the different equipment used by an air conditioning system.
c. Describe the function of summer air-conditioning system showing the block diagram.
d. A furnace handles $1,000\text{ cu ft/min (cfm)}$ of all outside air ($40^\circ\text{F}$ dry bulb and $40\%$ relative humidity). The air is heated and humidified and leaves the furnace at $110^\circ\text{F}$ (dry bulb). This air is supplied to the conditioned space where a condition of $70^\circ\text{F}$ and $50\%$ relative humidity is maintained.
   i) How many gallons of water per hour must the humidifier use to give the desired room condition?
   ii) What is the output of the furnace in Btu per hour?

**8.** `[(2+2+3+2=9)]`
a. Write the difference between an air blower and an air compressor.
b. What is a gas turbine? Explain the different methods of improving the efficiency of a simple gas turbine.
c. Briefly discuss the working principle of a reaction turbine with the necessary figure.
d. A gas turbine plant receive air at $1\text{ bar}$ and $290\text{ K}$, compressing it to a pressure of $5\text{ bar}$. The air temperature after compression reaches $1000\text{ K}$. Determine the thermal efficiency of the turbine, assuming the specific heat ratio ($\gamma$) for air is $1.4$.
