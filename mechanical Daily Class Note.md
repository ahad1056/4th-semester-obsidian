## Thermodynamic Processes

- Different thermodynamic processes are listed: Isotherm, Adiabatic, and Isobaric.
    
- Isothermic process: $\Delta T = 0$, which means $\Delta Q = \Delta W$.
    
- Adiabatic process: $\Delta Q = 0$, which leads to $-\Delta W = \Delta U$.
    
- Isobaric process: $\Delta P = 0$, resulting in $\Delta W = P \Delta V$.
    
- Isochoric process: $\Delta V = 0$.
    
    - Because $\Delta W = P \Delta V$, if $\Delta V = 0$, then $\Delta W = 0$.
        
    - Therefore, $\Delta Q = \Delta U$.
        

## Work and Heat Capacity

- Work done by an expanding gas is mentioned.
 - ![[mechanical Daily Class Note-1794671268457.webp|87x146]] 
    
- Heat Capacity ($C$): Defined as Heat ($Q$) divided by Temp change ($\Delta \theta$), so $C = \frac{Q}{\Delta \theta}$.
    
- Specific Heat Capacity ($S$): Defined as Heat ($Q$) divided by (Temp change ($\Delta \theta$) $\times$ mass ($m$)).
    
    - $S = \frac{Q}{m \Delta \theta}$.
        
    - Therefore, $S = \frac{C}{m}$.
        
- Molar specific heat: Includes $C_p$ and $C_v$.

## Class Notes: Thermodynamics - Specific Heat Relations

## 📌 Derivation: Relation between $C_p$ & $C_v$
The primary focus of this note is proving Mayer's relation: **$C_p - C_v = R$**.

### Initial Equations
- Heat formulas: $Q = ms\Delta\theta$ and $Q = ml_f$.
- For 1 mole of gas ($m=1$) at constant volume, taking specific heat $s = C_v$ and temperature change $\Delta\theta = dt$, the heat is defined as: $Q_1 = 1 \cdot C_v dt$.

### Side Work (Ideal Gas Law)
- $PV = nRT$
- For $n=1$, taking the derivative with respect to temperature: $P dV = R dt$
- Rearranging gives: $\frac{P dV}{dt} = R$

### Main Proof Steps
From the first law of thermodynamics (at constant pressure, $dQ = dU + dW$, so $C_p dT = C_v dT + P dV$):
1. $C_p dT = C_v dT + P dV$
2. $C_p dT - C_v dT = P dV$
3. $(C_p - C_v) = P \frac{dV}{dt}$
4. Substituting $\frac{P dV}{dt} = R$ into the equation gives the final relation:
   **$C_p - C_v = R$**

## Class Notes: Mechanical Engineering - 2nd Law of Thermodynamics (Nov 22)

## 🔄 Fundamental Thermodynamic Concepts
- **Reversible and Irreversible Processes:** Study of processes that can be reversed without leaving any trace on the surroundings versus natural processes that cannot.
- **Cyclic Process:** A series of thermodynamic processes in which a system eventually returns to its initial state.
- **Heat Reservoir and Heat Engine:** *(Corrected from "resurver")* Concepts of thermal energy reservoirs (high-temperature source and low-temperature sink) and devices that convert heat energy into mechanical work.

---
