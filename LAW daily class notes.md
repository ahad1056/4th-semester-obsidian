## Class Notes: Law (Oct 17) - The Contract Act 1872

**Book Reference:** Mercantile Law

## 📖 Definition of a Contract
- **Core Principle:** Contract = Agreement + lawfully enforceable.

## ⚖️ Essential Elements of a Contract
To be valid, a contract must fulfill the following criteria:
- Offer & Acceptance
- Intention
- Lawful consideration (written as "lawfull considaration")
- Capacity of party
- Consent (written as "Concent")
- Certainty

## 🏢 Example Scenario / Concept
**Project:** Solar panel project
- **Total Cost:** Taka - 25 lakh
- **Capacity:** 50 kW
- **Advanced Payment:** 10 lakh
- **Timeline/Status:** Within 2 month, work paused (written as "pused")
