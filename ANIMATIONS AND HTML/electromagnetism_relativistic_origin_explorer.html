Biot-Savart & Ampère's Law

When voltage is applied, free electrons drift through the metal lattice. Moving charges disturb the electromagnetic field, generating concentric circular magnetic field lines around the conductor.

👍 Right-Hand Grip Rule: Point your right thumb along conventional current (I). Your fingers curl in the direction of B.

In real copper wires, drift speed is ~1 mm/s, but because electric forces are immensely strong, even tiny Lorentz contraction produces a noticeable magnetic force.

Einstein's Relativistic Explanation

In the Lab Frame, positive ions and drifting electrons have equal spacing. The wire is electrically neutral, yet the moving positive test charge (+q) is pulled toward the wire by what we call the Magnetic Force.
